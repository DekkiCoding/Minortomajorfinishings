About Us

Minor to Major Finishings, led by Kristina Majors, is a Manitoba-based finishing company dedicated to delivering high-quality craftsmanship across residential and commercial projects. Serving Winnipeg and surrounding areas, we specialize in transforming spaces with reliable, detail-focused work that combines skill, precision, and pride in every project we take on.

From interior and exterior finishing to drywall, taping, painting, wood refinishing, and full renovation services, we provide a complete one-stop solution for clients looking to improve or restore their spaces. Whether it’s a small update or a full-scale renovation, our goal is to deliver clean, lasting results that meet the highest standards of workmanship.

We take pride in being dependable, professional, and easy to work with, building strong relationships with clients throughout Manitoba by consistently delivering quality finishes that speak for themselves.
